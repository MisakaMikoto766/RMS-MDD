# Data Governance Statement

This statement documents the data sources, permissions and use conditions, redistribution basis, de-identification status, ethical determination, and manual quality review associated with RMS-MDD.

## 1. Source Datasets and Permissions

RMS-MDD is constructed from three publicly released medical dialogue datasets: IMCS-21, DXY, and MDD.

### IMCS-dialog

IMCS-dialog is derived from the publicly released IMCS-21 dataset. Its restructuring, transformation, and redistribution were performed with permission from the original dataset authors. The transformed data are provided for non-commercial academic research.

### DXY-dialog

DXY-dialog is derived from the publicly released DXY dataset. Its restructuring, transformation, and redistribution were performed with permission from the original dataset authors. The transformed data are provided for non-commercial academic research.

### MDD-dialog

MDD-dialog is derived from the publicly released MDD data and the MDDial dialogue resource constructed from them. Its construction and redistribution follow the original MDD terms of use. Accordingly, MDD-dialog remains subject to the original MDD restrictions for non-commercial academic research.

## 2. Redistribution Basis

Public availability is distinct from redistribution permission. For IMCS-dialog and DXY-dialog, redistribution of the transformed dialogues is based on permission from the original dataset authors. For MDD-dialog, redistribution follows the academic and non-commercial use conditions of the original MDD data.

RMS-MDD does not grant additional rights beyond those provided by the original data sources. Any repository-level software license applies to the code only and does not override restrictions governing source-derived dialogue data.

## 3. De-identification

The source datasets used by RMS-MDD were obtained from publicly released, de-identified versions provided by their original developers. This study accessed no direct personal identifiers and introduced no additional patient-level information during dataset transformation.

## 4. Ethical and Institutional-Review Determination

This work involves secondary analysis and transformation of publicly available, de-identified datasets. It did not involve participant recruitment, interaction with patients or healthcare providers, collection of new clinical data, or access to identifiable private information. Accordingly, no additional institutional ethics approval was required for this secondary use.

## 5. Manual Quality Review

The manual validation reported in the manuscript reviewed 150 dialogue entries, distributed evenly across the three transformed datasets:

- 50 entries from DXY-dialog;
- 50 entries from IMCS-dialog;
- 50 entries from MDD-dialog.

The review assessed the reliability of inherited symptom and disease labels on a sampled subset rather than re-annotating the full benchmark. No additional personal information was collected or inferred during this review.

## 6. Scope

Users remain responsible for complying with the terms, permissions, and restrictions of the corresponding source datasets. This statement does not replace the original source-dataset terms.
