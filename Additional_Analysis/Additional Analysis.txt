These findings demonstrate that each component of SPD contributes meaningfully to overall performance. Retrieval with three to five passages provides the best balance between information richness and noise. Structured symptom-state prompts improve grounding and reduce ambiguity. The DSL loss is important for addressing long-tail symptoms and maintaining diagnostic consistency.
